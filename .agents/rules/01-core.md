# Core workshop rules

- Patch in place.
- Do not rebuild from scratch.
- Do not overwrite approved assets.
- Do not auto-approve any asset.
- Treat approved exports and the manifest as production data.
- Prefer small, reviewable edits over broad rewrites.
- End every task with a proof artifact.
- If you cannot prove a claim, do not state it as done.
